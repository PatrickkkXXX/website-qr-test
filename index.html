This scam is called quishing (QR + phishing). A QR code hides the web address, so you can't tell where it leads until you're already there.

Red flags

  • A sticker pasted over the real QR code on a parking meter, poster, or restaurant table
  • A QR code in a text about a "failed delivery", "locked account", or unpaid fine
  • Urgent wording: "Scan within 24 hours or lose access"
  • Page asks you to log in, enter an OTP, or pay right after scanning
  • Web address is misspelled or doesn't match the company name

Safe habits

  • Read the link preview your camera shows before you tap it
  • Only scan codes from sources you trust
  • For your bank, GCash, or Maya â open the official app instead
  • Never share OTPs or PINs with anyone, even "customer support"
  • Only install apps from the official App Store or Google Play

If you already entered your details

  • Change that password now, plus any account using the same one.
  • Call your bank or e-wallet's official hotline and ask them to lock the account.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) everywhere.
  • Report to the PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group or CICC hotline 1326.
